---
title: How do I extract the PARAM.SFO from the SaveData?
description: How to export the PARAM.SFO from a PS4 save
---

## Steps

1. To extract the `PARAM.SFO` from a Savedata you need **Apollo Save Tool** installed on your jailbroken PS4 (5.05+).
2. Go to `HDD Saves`, open the Savedata and choose the `Export savegame to zip` option.
3. A ZIP file will be created at `/data/apollo/export`, and the SFO file will be at:  `/CUSA00000-0000001/savegame0/sce_sys/param.sfo`
